up scored on the search seasons 2018/19-2022/23 and the unseen seasons 2023/24-2025/26, model
only, Bet365 prices, flat 100; "luck control" = the same count with the model's chances shuffled.

bars were found on) and reports/window_both_above_50_calibrated (calibrated chances, 2019/20 on).

## Decisions

* The three match-winner bars stay as they are: still profitable in both periods on the
  5-league model, and so far this season.
* No golden bars in the Bundesliga or Ligue 1: shuffled chances find as many "profitable"
  windows as the model does, so anything found there would be luck.
* The over/under bar stays until its 100-settled-pick review (rule set 22 Nov); it is at 0% in
  the search seasons and losing this season, so expect it to be dropped then.
* Fixed: since calibration went live (ADR 0020) the bars were being checked on calibrated
  chances, a different scale from the one they were searched on. They now use the model's
  unadjusted chances (betting/golden.py `unadjusted`), as searched. On calibrated chances the
  Premier League and Serie A winner bars would not have been found at all.
